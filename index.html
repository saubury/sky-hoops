SKYHOOPS
Real Sydney air traffic. Imaginary hoops. Questionable air-traffic control.
  1. Press and hold on a plane (or its label) to wind up a hoop.
  2. The longer you hold, the further ahead it lands — and the more it is worth.
  3. Release to throw. If the real flight goes through it, you score. If the pilot turns… you don't.
  4. Chain hits for a streak multiplier, fill the bar for FEVER, and chase the golden plane for ×3.
  5. Answer tower calls — "hoop a wide-body", "hoop an overseas airline" — for ×3. The labels only give you a callsign and a type code, so know your fleet.
